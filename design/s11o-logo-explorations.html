Eleven typographic directions for s11o

Four glyphs, no natural pictogram — this wants to be a wordmark. Comps 01–06 mine the site's Xbox 360 design system: brand green hsl(95 62% 45%), dark glass, glossy caps, Segoe-era type, Courier accents. Comps 07–11 step outside the console into the wider 2000s — band merch, videogame title screens, and the tech of the era.

The Orb — dashboard-native

s11

The terminal o becomes the glossy green sphere — a direct homage to the 360 boot orb and your card tiles. Heavy lowercase Segoe keeps it era-correct. Warm, friendly, instantly reads as "the Xbox site guy."

Recommendation

Primary mark: 03 Pause — it's the only direction that's genuinely yours (the silo reading + pause glyph exist nowhere else), survives one-color and 16px, and won't date if you ever re-skin the site. Keep the green bar as the single brand accent.

In-world voice: 01 Orb or 05 Plate — use inside the dashboard and on the /card page where the Xbox fiction is the point. The pause mark is the passport; the orb/plate are the costume.

Handle contexts (social bios, commit signatures): the 04 terminal treatment of s11o_online as styled text, no artwork needed.

Dark horses from the second wave: 12 and 10. The o_o face is the strongest single idea on this page — discovered inside the domain, not designed onto it — and it composes with almost every other direction (a pause-mark wordmark whose favicon is o_o; an LCD that blinks o_o). The 5110 pun is equally ownable but demands its yellow-green backlight; pick it only if the identity should read phone-era tech rather than console-era. 11 Die-cut is the physical-world kit regardless of which mark wins.
